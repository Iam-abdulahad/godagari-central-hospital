About Us

About Godagari Central Hospital

Godagari Central Hospital is a government-registered private healthcare facility in Srimantapur, Godagari, serving the greater Rajshahi region with reliable, affordable and dignified medical care.

Our Mission: to deliver quality, patient-centered healthcare through skilled professionals and modern technology.
Our Vision: to be the most trusted healthcare destination in northern Bangladesh.
